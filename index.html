Inventory Scan

How it works

  1. Start a listTally Sheet, Weekly Inventory, or Customer Pickup. Your name and the date fill in automatically.
  2. Scan each itemPoint the camera at the barcode; it beeps and moves on. Use Type it or Photo for hard labels. On a computer, type or use a USB scanner.
  3. Fix anythingTap any saved item to edit it. After a save or delete, tap Undo in the message at the bottom.
  4. Export and back upExport PDF, Excel, or CSV from a list. Back up all lists from Settings, since they live only on this device.
